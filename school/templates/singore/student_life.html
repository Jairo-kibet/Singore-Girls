Student Experience

Building Character Beyond the Classroom

At Singore Girls Senior School, student life is an important part of the learning experience. We believe that education extends beyond academic lessons to include personal development, creativity, leadership and responsible citizenship.

Through clubs, societies, sports, games and spiritual activities, students are encouraged to explore their interests, discover their abilities and develop meaningful relationships with others.

These experiences help nurture confidence, teamwork, discipline, resilience and a strong sense of belonging, preparing learners to become responsible and productive members of society.

SPIRITUAL GUIDANCE

Chaplaincy Department

Faith & Spiritual Growth

Welcome to the Chaplaincy Department

The Chaplaincy Department provides spiritual guidance and encouragement to students and staff, nurturing a community founded on faith, integrity, compassion and mutual respect.

Through prayer meetings, Bible study, worship, fellowship and outreach activities, students are encouraged to grow spiritually and develop values that guide their daily lives.

The chaplaincy also provides opportunities for students to strengthen friendships, support one another and develop leadership through service. It encourages learners to live responsibly, respect others and contribute positively to the school and wider community.

We believe that spiritual development is an important part of holistic education, helping students build character and find purpose as they prepare for the future.
