Building a Culture of Academic Excellence

Dear Students, Parents, Teachers and Visitors,

It is my pleasure to welcome you to Singore Girls Senior School, where education is viewed as a foundation for personal growth, responsible leadership and lifelong success. We are committed to creating an academic environment that challenges, inspires and empowers every learner to achieve her full potential.

As the Director of Studies, I oversee the coordination and implementation of our academic programmes, working closely with our dedicated teaching staff to promote effective curriculum delivery, quality instruction and consistent academic progress.

We recognise that every learner has unique abilities and aspirations. Through continuous assessment, academic mentorship, guidance and appropriate interventions, we seek to identify individual needs and provide opportunities for every student to excel.

I encourage our learners to embrace discipline, perseverance, curiosity and a genuine commitment to learning. Academic success is a shared responsibility, strengthened through the partnership between students, teachers, parents and the wider school community.

Together, let us continue building a learning environment where excellence is pursued, potential is nurtured and every learner is prepared to make a meaningful contribution to society.

Director of Studies
