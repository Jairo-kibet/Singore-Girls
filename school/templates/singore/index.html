Nurturing Excellence, Discipline and Future Leaders

singore Girls Senior School is committed to providing quality education in an environment that promotes academic excellence, discipline, integrity and personal growth. Message from the Principal

Inspiring Excellence, Shaping Tomorrow's Leaders

Welcome to Singore Girls Senior School. It is my pleasure to welcome you to our institution, where we are committed to nurturing young minds and developing responsible, disciplined and knowledgeable individuals. We believe that education is the foundation upon which strong character, meaningful opportunities and a successful future are built.

At Singore Girls Senior School, we strive to provide a supportive and inspiring learning environment where every student is encouraged to discover their potential, pursue academic excellence and develop essential life skills. Beyond classroom learning, we emphasize discipline, integrity, respect, creativity and personal responsibility as important values that shape our learners.

Our commitment is to prepare students not only for academic achievement but also for the challenges and opportunities of an ever-changing world. Through quality teaching, dedicated staff, meaningful mentorship and a strong school community, we seek to produce confident individuals who can make positive contributions to society.

I invite parents, guardians, alumni and all stakeholders to join us in this important journey of transforming lives through education. Together, we can continue building a culture of excellence and shaping a generation of responsible leaders.

The Principal

Academic Results

KCSE 2025: A Mean Grade of B+

ingore Girls Senior School continues to demonstrate its commitment to academic excellence. In the 2025 KCSE examinations, 362 out of 377 candidates scored C+ and above, qualifying for direct university entry.

These results reflect the dedication of our students, teachers, school management and parents in building a strong culture of academic achievement.
