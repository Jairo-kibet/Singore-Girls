THE JOURNEY OF EXCELLENCE

Rooted in Heritage, Focused on the Future

Singore Girls Senior School traces its roots to 1st August 1965, when it was established as a Harambee day secondary school under the sponsorship of the African Inland Mission (AIM), now the African Inland Church (AIC).

The school began with 40 learners, comprising 30 boys and 10 girls. What started as a community educational initiative gradually developed into an established public secondary school, driven by a shared commitment to providing quality education and creating opportunities for learners.

Over the years, the institution experienced significant administrative and structural development. It was taken over by the Government in 1980 and officially registered as a public school in 1981. In 1996, it was re-registered as a four-stream mixed secondary school.

A major milestone came in January 2006, when the institution transitioned into a boys-only school. Since then, it has continued to build its identity around academic achievement, discipline, leadership and holistic development.

Today, Singore Girls Senior School continues to build on its rich heritage, preparing learners with the knowledge, values and skills needed to become responsible leaders and productive members of society.
