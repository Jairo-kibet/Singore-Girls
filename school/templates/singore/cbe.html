Learning Through Knowledge and Practice

Understanding CBE

What is Competency-Based Education?

Competency-Based Education (CBE) is a learner-centred approach that focuses on what students know, understand and can demonstrate through practical application of knowledge and skills.

Unlike approaches that place greater emphasis on memorization and time spent in the classroom, CBE emphasizes the development and demonstration of competencies. Learners are encouraged to apply what they learn, think critically, work collaboratively and find solutions to everyday challenges.

In Kenya, the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) provides the framework for developing these competencies throughout basic education, including Senior School. The approach prepares learners for further education, employment, innovation and responsible citizenship.

The CBE Approach

Core Principles of Competency-Based Learning

CBE promotes meaningful learning through practical experiences, learner participation and continuous development of abilities.

Competency Mastery

Learners develop and demonstrate the knowledge, skills and attitudes required to perform tasks effectively.

Learner-Centred Learning

Learning activities recognize individual abilities, interests, talents and different learning needs.

Practical Assessment

Learners demonstrate their understanding through projects, practical activities, assignments and other assessments.

Continuous Feedback

Regular feedback helps learners identify strengths, address challenges and improve their performance.

Kenya's CBC Framework

The Seven Core Competencies

The Kenyan curriculum develops seven key competencies that equip learners with the abilities and values needed to succeed in life.

01
Communication and Collaboration

Expressing ideas clearly, listening to others and working effectively in teams.

02
Critical Thinking and Problem Solving

Analysing situations, making informed decisions and finding solutions to challenges.

03
Imagination and Creativity

Developing original ideas, exploring possibilities and creating innovative solutions.

04
Citizenship

Demonstrating responsibility, respect, patriotism and active participation in society.

05
Learning to Learn

Developing curiosity, independent learning habits and the ability to acquire new knowledge.

06
Self-Efficacy

Building confidence, resilience, self-awareness and belief in one's ability to achieve goals.

07
Digital Literacy

Using digital technologies responsibly to access information, communicate and innovate.

Measuring Progress

How Are Learners Assessed?

Assessment under the competency-based approach goes beyond traditional written examinations. It considers the knowledge, practical abilities, values and skills that learners demonstrate throughout the learning process.

Teachers use different assessment methods to monitor progress, identify learning needs and provide feedback. This allows learners to demonstrate their abilities in different ways and work towards achieving the expected learning outcomes.

Classwork and assignments
Practical activities and projects
Portfolios and evidence of learning
Teacher observations and feedback
National assessments
